# Archive instead of delete

No Sales record is removed from the database by a user. Where a Manager could delete one (a Lead, a Booking, an Opportunity, an Account), they archive it instead; the record keeps everything it holds and can be restored. What the API still removes physically carries no history of its own: a Site (an address of an Account), a Quotation draft its author discards before it is sent, and a saved board query of the user's own. (The `delete_visit` service, which no endpoint called, went with the Account's delete.)

## The rule

- **Who.** A CRM Manager, as the delete was. Sales users and Engineers see no archive action and no restore.
- **What archiving does.** The record is marked archived, with when and by whom, by a write of those columns alone (no full save: none of the record's other validations run for a flag flip). The archive and the restore each leave a trace on the record (a comment on a Lead or a Booking, a history row on an Opportunity): who did what, and when, after the restore as well. It leaves every list (unless the list's archive filter asks for it), every picker, and the figures, warnings and reminders: its own (a record of another kind that names it, a Visit or a Quotation say, keeps counting as its own; the sections below say which). It still opens at its own URL, read-only, with a notice; a Manager restores it from there. Restoring brings every field back as it was. Both move the record's `modified`, so a page opened before holds an older version and its save is refused.
- **Concurrency.** The archive and the restore read the record under its row lock (a locking read); every write on the record checks its version (`lock_document`, or Frappe's own `check_if_latest`, which the API answers as `VERSION_CONFLICT`), so an archive committed after a client read is never written over.
- **Nothing new under an archived record.** An archived record cannot be edited, converted or moved on, and no new record is created under it.
- **The list filter.** Each list has a "归档状态 / Archive state" filter: active (the default), archived, or all. Pages read the filter as `archiveState=active|archived|all`.
- **Native lists.** The lists and pickers above are the CRM's own. Frappe's own (the Desk's list and report views, its REST list, its link search) are left as they were: they put no condition on the archive and show an archived record to whoever may read it, as they show an archived Contact (a list, a report or the REST list shows it unless one filters on its archive field, CRM Archived on a Lead or an Account, Archived on a Booking or an Opportunity). Leaving archived records out of them (a condition on their list hook, which the record's own page does not read) is a product decision for later.
- **Nothing cascades except what is said below.** Records that name an archived one keep naming it.

## Per record

| Record | Archive refused when | Archived with it | State |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lead | it is converted (it is its Account's history) | — | done |
| Booking | its source is an Opportunity (cancel it instead), or it is completed (a service delivered, an order converted to an Installed Tool): the delete refused the first, and most of the second (those an Installed Tool or a notice names) | — | done |
| Opportunity | a Booking of its own is not cancelled (an order the deal stands behind; the delete was refused for it too), or a Demo Request or demo test of its own is in progress (work under way on it) | its Quotations, frozen with it (its stakeholders, actions, history, validations, plans, post-mortems, status records, notices and warning handling all stay on it) | done |
| Account | an Opportunity is open under it (at an open stage, not archived itself), a Booking is in progress (confirmed or pending, not archived), or a Demo Request or demo test of its deals is in progress | its Quotations, frozen with it (its Sites and Contacts leave the lists and the pickers with it and take no change; they are not marked, and come back with it) | done |
| Contact | — (archived through its own page, as before; not while its Account is archived) | — | as before |
| Visit | never deletable; no archive (the `delete_visit` service, which no endpoint called, is gone) | — | — |

## Lead

- API: `leads.archive_lead(id)` and `leads.unarchive_lead(id)` (POST, Manager); `leads.list_leads` takes `archiveState`; the Lead carries `archived`, and `archivedAt` and `archivedBy` while archived.
- An archived Lead is refused an edit and a conversion (`INVALID_TRANSITION`, "a Manager can restore it from the archive"); a converted Lead is refused the archive: one whose status says so, the CRM's or ERPNext's own, or which a conversion left a trace on whatever its status (one of its conversion columns; a Customer naming it as the Lead it was made from, ERPNext's own mark of a conversion, from which ERPNext sets its status on the Customer's insert and whenever it works the Lead's status out again (the Lead's save, an ERPNext Opportunity or Quotation of its own), not when the link is given later; a deal naming it as its source; a conversion in a deal's stage history: a legacy conversion, or one repaired in the Desk, can leave the status, or any one trace, behind). ERPNext's own conversion in the Desk (a Customer made from the Lead) reads no archive flag: it is not refused for an archived Lead, which stays archived.
- Every figure and warning reads a Lead only through its conversion (its column naming a deal, or the deal's stage history), which an archived Lead has none of: Review's new qualified leads (and the targets counted on them), the board's last activity of a deal and the warnings' stagnation (a deal's last activity). Plan reads no Lead (its lead commitments are a Manager's numbers); no reminder reads a Lead.
- ERPNext keeps a Lead's email unique over every Lead (CRM Settings `allow_lead_duplication_based_on_emails` is off), archived ones included: a new Lead with the email of an archived one is refused (`CONFLICT`, "Another Lead already uses this email address."); a Manager restores the archived Lead or the user picks another email. The Lead's owner is not told of an archive (the delete did not tell them either).
- Data: custom fields `custom_crm_archived` (Check), `custom_crm_archived_at` (Datetime) and `custom_crm_archived_by` (Link User) on Lead, installed by the custom-field sync on migrate.

## Booking

- API: `bookings.archive_booking(id)` and `bookings.unarchive_booking(id)` (POST, Manager); `bookings.list_bookings` and its summary take `archiveState`; `bookings.get_booking(id)` reads one (the Booking page reads by id, since an archived Booking is not in the pipeline); the Booking carries `archived`, and `archivedAt` and `archivedBy` while archived.
- Only an Upsell Booking that is not completed is archived. An Opportunity Booking is the PO record behind its Opportunity (the live booking, rollback and loss rules rely on it) and is only ever cancelled; a completed Booking (a service delivered, an order converted to an Installed Tool, which names it) is the record of a delivered order (`INVALID_TRANSITION` for both; the delete refused the first, and most completed ones: those an Installed Tool or a notice names).
- The Booking page reads its record by id; a Booking the reader cannot see is "not found" to them, as it was when it was not in their pipeline.
- An archived Booking is refused every change (`INVALID_TRANSITION`, "a Manager can restore it from the archive"): an edit, the actual PO date and the CNY amount, a service completion, a conversion to an Installed Tool. It is out of the Booking list and summary (active by default; the pipeline, the dashboard and the Contact 360 read the list), the Account 360 (which reads the list unpaginated, active ones alone), the deadline reminders and the CNY backfill report. Review counts only an Opportunity's own Bookings, which are never archived.
- Data: fields `archived` (Check), `archived_at` (Datetime) and `archived_by` (Link User) on CRM Booking, installed by the doctype sync on migrate.

## Opportunity

- API: `opportunities.archive_strategy_opportunity(id)` and `opportunities.unarchive_strategy_opportunity(id)` (POST, Manager); `opportunities.list_strategy_opportunities` takes `archiveState`; the Opportunity carries `archived`, and `archivedAt` and `archivedBy` while archived; its history holds an `archived` row for each archive and restore.
- Refused (`INVALID_TRANSITION`) while a Booking of its own is not cancelled (cancel the Booking first; a completed order cannot be cancelled, so a delivered deal stays, as it could not be deleted), a Demo Request that has not ended (cancel it first) or a demo test that has not ended (finish or terminate it first). The archive reads them under the Opportunity's lock, as committed by then, so none of them is in progress once it is archived: no order to deliver, no Demo Request in a review, no demo test running, and nothing left to remind of. An internal validation or a quotation approval waiting is frozen with it (no decision until the restore), not waited for; an RFQ goes on up to its approval, which is refused (an RFQ has no end the archive could wait for).
- An archived Opportunity is refused every change (`INVALID_TRANSITION`, "cannot be changed"): an edit, a stage action (budget, internal validation, PO, marking lost), a rollback, a stakeholder, an action, a recovery plan or action, a post-mortem (its loss category too), a status record, a scorecard link, its red flags or the Blue Sheet's suggested level (its page offers neither), a warning's next step. Its Quotations are frozen with it: no draft saved or discarded, no new version, no submission, approval (either way) or publication, no customer decision, no send plan or follow-up, until the restore; the Quotation says its Opportunity is archived (`opportunityArchived`), and its page offers none of those writes. An RFQ approval, which would move the Opportunity on, is refused at that step. It takes no new record naming it ("takes no new record"): a Visit, a Demo Request, a Quotation (created or linked), an RFQ, a Booking, an Event (created, or made to name it); each of those creations reads the flag again under the Opportunity's lock (a Quotation, created or linked, before its row is written; an RFQ after its row and its files are written, which a refusal takes back out, the files' bytes included), so an archive in flight is waited for and one committed since the first read is seen. A Quotation's publication reads it under the lock once its PDF is rendered and the PDF's content lock taken (no file's content lock is waited for while the Opportunity's row is held), before it writes anything. Its own Bookings (cancelled, since it is archived) take no change either. Its Demo Requests have all ended (the archive waits for them): they take no step, and keep their comments and Word copies as any ended one does. It is out of the Opportunity list and board, the Opportunity Board (which has no archive filter), the pipeline (the Account and Contact 360 with it), the dashboard (which reads the deals and their actions itself), the Account's figures, the cockpit (Review's figures of the deal: its stage, amount, stages, actions, plans, probability and Lead conversion; Warnings; Plan's open deals and actions, and its Quotations to send and their follow-ups, as a closed deal's; a quotation of its own is no expiry warning) and the warning notices. The Visits, Quotations (their follow-ups done too), Events and demo tests that name it are records of their own: they keep counting where they count (Review's activity facts: the Visits, the Quotations and their follow-ups done, the demo tests), and a planned Visit or Event naming it, the Account's, stays in Plan (an Event naming it is still kept up, as a Visit is).
- Notices and warning handling already written about it stay (they are the users' history; a notice opens the read-only page). The warning job reads the slip and legacy stage-6 rows again under the Opportunity's lock before it writes a notice, so a parent archived after the job's snapshot gets none of those; a lost, overdue, quotation-expiry or stage-6-with-history notice of the job's snapshot can still be written once.
- Data: fields `archived` (Check), `archived_at` (Datetime) and `archived_by` (Link User) on CRM Opportunity, installed by the doctype sync on migrate.

## Account

- API: `accounts.archive_account(id)` and `accounts.unarchive_account(id)` (POST, Manager); `accounts.list_accounts` takes `archiveState`; the Account carries `archived`, and `archivedAt` and `archivedBy` while archived; the Contact 360 carries `accountArchived` and `accountOwner` (the Account's owner, which the page compares with its reader: the Account list leaves an archived Account out).
- Refused while business is open under it (`INVALID_TRANSITION`): an Opportunity at an open stage that is not archived itself ("close or archive the Opportunity first"), a Booking that is confirmed or pending and not archived ("cancel or complete the Booking first"), or a Demo Request or a demo test that has not ended, of a deal it has now (an Opportunity moved to another Account takes its demo work along) or its own without a deal ("cancel the Demo Request first", "finish or terminate the demo test first"; a deal lost while its demo was under way is closed, its demo work is not). The archive holds the Account's row and reads the Opportunities and Bookings under their locks, so an action in flight on one of them is waited for and seen; the Demo Requests and their tests as committed by then (none is started while the Account's row is held). What is closed stays under the archived Account: a deal won or lost, a cancelled or completed Booking, an archived Opportunity or Booking, the Visits, the ended Demo Requests and demo tests, the RFQs, the Installed Tools (the equipment is serviced whatever the Account's standing in the CRM); its Quotations are frozen with it.
- An archived Account takes no change ("An archived Account cannot be changed"): its profile, its Sites (edited, removed), its Contacts (edited, merged, archived) and their files (an upload or a removal reads the Contact's Accounts again as committed, under the Account's row: a Contact moved to another Account meanwhile is refused as changed, `VERSION_CONFLICT`), its Quotations (a Quotation answers to its deal's Account now, or to its own when it has no deal: no draft saved or discarded, no new version, no submission, approval or publication, no customer decision, no send plan or follow-up, no link; the Quotation says its Account is archived, `accountArchived`, and its page offers none of those); nothing moves out of it (a Contact, a closed deal, a cancelled order, an Event: moved out, one could be reopened under another Account). Nothing new is started under it ("An archived Account takes no new record"): a Contact, a Site, an Opportunity, a Booking, a Visit, an Event, a Demo Request, a Quotation, a Lead converted into it, a Contact, an Opportunity, a Booking or an Event moved under it, the Booking an internal validation would create. Nothing closed under it is reopened or restored ("Nothing is reopened or restored under an archived Account"): the rollback of any of its deals (lost, or won with its order cancelled), a replacement order for a won deal, a cancelled Booking put back in progress, an Event done or cancelled planned again, the restore of an archived Opportunity or Booking of its own; a Manager restores the Account first. Otherwise its closed Opportunities and cancelled Bookings keep their own rules: their details may still be corrected (the editors read the Account's own Contacts by its id, as the pickers leave them out). A Visit planned before the archive is still held, its minutes written and reviewed, and an Event under it is still kept up (its dates and notes, done or cancelled, its deal cleared; not given another deal); an RFQ of a closed deal goes on up to what its Opportunity's own rules allow.
- Concurrency: a service that starts a record under an Account, or changes one of the Account's own, reads the Account's archive flag under a lock on its row, taken before the rows it shares with the Account's archive (its Opportunities and Bookings), the order the archive takes them in, so an archive in flight is waited for and a record started after it sees the archive. The flag a name reads is the one of the row the database matched it to, in the statement that locks it (its collation sets case, accents, width and the like aside, so a name spelt otherwise than its record reads that record's). The lock is shared (`LOCK IN SHARE MODE`), except for a writer that saves a Contact, an Address or a Lead of the Account, whose save takes the Account's row `FOR UPDATE` in its hook (`permissions.scope._lock_customer_groups`): that writer takes it `FOR UPDATE` from the start, so a lock is never raised from shared to exclusive (two writers holding it shared would each wait for the other). So does an Opportunity's edit, whose write scope takes its Account's row `FOR UPDATE` (`opportunity_status._lock_write_scope`, as a status record's does): a move takes both Accounts' rows so. A record moved between two Accounts takes both rows in one statement, in the order the database sorts their names (the hook's order). A status record of an Opportunity takes the Account's row before the Opportunity's, and refuses one moved meanwhile; so do the restores of an Opportunity and a Booking. A Contact file upload takes its files' content locks before the Account's row, as a status record does. A writer that locks a User (an Opportunity's edit its owners', an Event's save its owner's, as a native rename or merge of the User holds it) takes it before any Account's row; a Lead's conversion takes its owner's User rows, which the Lead's save takes in its hook (`permissions.scope.revoke_lead_shares_entering_crm`), after the Lead's row and before the Account's. An Event's save takes the User's row, the Event's, its owner's roles when it is assigned, its Accounts' (shared, by one statement), then its Opportunities' (likewise, exclusive), each named as the database has it (its collation matches a name without regard to case, accents, width and the like: a variant names that record). Every Quotation path takes a Quotation's rows before the Account's, then the Opportunity's: a writer its own row (a publication renders its PDF in memory and takes its content lock first; a send plan or a follow-up takes its Accounts' rows shared, the one it is addressed to and the one it answers to, by one statement), a link its Quotation's row, a creation none (it counts its date's reference last, on its date's row in tabSeries, which locks no Quotation); and none writes anything before the Account's and the Opportunity's rows. None waits for the Account's row holding a row it wrote, which a holder of that row could be waiting for behind an exclusive request queued on it (a Contact's edit, a status record, the archive), since such a request holds back every shared request after it; once both rows are held, writers can still wait for each other's writes (a naming row, the index gap of neighbouring versions' lines, a catalog key), as before, which the database detects, refusing one. A submission numbers its approval from a read without a lock (its Quotation's row held), through an index; the daily expiry reads a Quotation's current versions under its row's lock and writes the status columns only. Under the Opportunity's lock, a deal moved to another Account since the first read is refused as changed. The archive reads the Account's Opportunities and Bookings under their row locks through the index on their `account` column, so it locks that Account's rows and the index gaps next to them, not the tables; the DocTypes declare that index (`search_index`), so Frappe's schema sync makes it and keeps it (and the app makes it after a migrate where the sync did not), and the archive names the column's single-column index, whatever its name.
- Out of the Account list (unless asked for), every Account picker (they read the list), the cockpit's Opportunity Board (its deals, closed or archived, with their totals and export), the dashboard (it reads the list), the cockpit (its figures and warnings are keyed on the Accounts it lists; a next step for the expiry warning of one of its Quotations is refused as no longer current), the Sites of every picker (`list_account_sites`) and the Contact list and pickers (a Contact of an archived Account is read through the Account's own page, its id asked for, and its own). The Account 360 still opens at its URL, read-only, with its Sites, Contacts and records.
- Duplicate names: an archived Account still counts as a name taken (the duplicate check names it; a Manager may rather restore it).
- Data: custom fields `custom_crm_archived` (Check), `custom_crm_archived_at` (Datetime) and `custom_crm_archived_by` (Link User) on Customer, installed by the custom-field sync on migrate; the `account` field of CRM Opportunity and CRM Booking indexed (`search_index`: Frappe's schema sync names the index `account` on a new table and `account_index` when it adds one to an existing table; it looks for one by the table's name alone, in every database of the server, so the app makes `account_index` after a migrate when the column has no single-column index; the same for `CRM Quotation Approval.quotation`, which a submission's read of its approvals goes through). The `delete_account` and `delete_visit` services and the shared `delete_manager_scoped_document` helper are gone.
