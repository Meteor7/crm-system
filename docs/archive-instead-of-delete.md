# Archive instead of delete

No Sales record is removed from the database by a user (once each record's PR is in: the table below says which are). Where a Manager could delete one (a Lead, a Booking, an Opportunity, an Account), they archive it instead; the record keeps everything it holds and can be restored. What the API still removes physically carries no history of its own: a Site (an address of an Account), a Quotation draft its author discards before it is sent, and a saved board query of the user's own. (`services/visits.py` still holds a `delete_visit` that no endpoint calls; it goes with the last of these PRs.)

## The rule

- **Who.** A CRM Manager, as the delete was. Sales users and Engineers see no archive action and no restore.
- **What archiving does.** The record is marked archived, with when and by whom, by a write of those columns alone (no full save: none of the record's other validations run for a flag flip). The archive and the restore each leave a trace on the record (a comment on a Lead or a Booking, a history row on an Opportunity): who did what, and when, after the restore as well. It leaves every list (unless the list's archive filter asks for it), every picker, and the figures, warnings and reminders: its own (a record of another kind that names it, a Visit or a Quotation say, keeps counting as its own; the sections below say which). It still opens at its own URL, read-only, with a notice; a Manager restores it from there. Restoring brings every field back as it was. Both move the record's `modified`, so a page opened before holds an older version and its save is refused.
- **Concurrency.** The archive and the restore read the record under its row lock (a locking read); every write on the record checks its version (`lock_document`, or Frappe's own `check_if_latest`, which the API answers as `VERSION_CONFLICT`), so an archive committed after a client read is never written over.
- **Nothing new under an archived record.** An archived record cannot be edited, converted or moved on, and no new record is created under it.
- **The list filter.** Each list has a "归档状态 / Archive state" filter: active (the default), archived, or all. Pages read the filter as `archiveState=active|archived|all`.
- **Native lists.** The lists and pickers above are the CRM's own. Frappe's own (the Desk's list and report views, its REST list, its link search) are left as they were: they put no condition on the archive and show an archived record to whoever may read it, as they show an archived Contact (a list, a report or the REST list shows it unless one filters on its archive field, CRM Archived on a Lead, Archived on a Booking or an Opportunity). Leaving archived records out of them (a condition on their list hook, which the record's own page does not read) is a product decision for later.
- **Nothing cascades except what is said below.** Records that name an archived one keep naming it.

## Per record

| Record | Archive refused when | Archived with it | State |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lead | it is converted (it is its Account's history) | — | done |
| Booking | its source is an Opportunity (cancel it instead), or it is completed (a service delivered, an order converted to an Installed Tool): the delete refused the first, and most of the second (those an Installed Tool or a notice names) | — | done |
| Opportunity | a Booking of its own is not cancelled (an order the deal stands behind; the delete was refused for it too), or a Demo Request or demo test of its own is in progress (work under way on it) | its Quotations, frozen with it (its stakeholders, actions, history, validations, plans, post-mortems, status records, notices and warning handling all stay on it) | done |
| Account | it has open Opportunities, live Bookings or planned Visits | its active Contacts, restored with it | next |
| Contact | — (archived through its own page, as before) | — | as before |
| Visit | never deletable; no archive | — | — |

## Lead

- API: `leads.archive_lead(id)` and `leads.unarchive_lead(id)` (POST, Manager); `leads.list_leads` takes `archiveState`; the Lead carries `archived`, and `archivedAt` and `archivedBy` while archived.
- An archived Lead is refused an edit and a conversion (`INVALID_TRANSITION`, "a Manager can restore it from the archive"); a converted Lead is refused the archive: one whose status says so, the CRM's or ERPNext's own, or which a conversion left a trace on whatever its status (one of its conversion columns; a Customer naming it as the Lead it was made from, ERPNext's own mark of a conversion, from which ERPNext sets its status on the Customer's insert and whenever it works the Lead's status out again (the Lead's save, an ERPNext Opportunity or Quotation of its own), not when the link is given later; a deal naming it as its source; a conversion in a deal's stage history: a legacy conversion, or one repaired in the Desk, can leave the status, or any one trace, behind). ERPNext's own conversion in the Desk (a Customer made from the Lead) reads no archive flag: it is not refused for an archived Lead, which stays archived.
- Every figure and warning reads a Lead only through its conversion (its column naming a deal, or the deal's stage history), which an archived Lead has none of: Review's new qualified leads (and the targets counted on them), the board's last activity of a deal and the warnings' stagnation (a deal's last activity). Plan reads no Lead (its lead commitments are a Manager's numbers); no reminder reads a Lead.
- ERPNext keeps a Lead's email unique over every Lead (CRM Settings `allow_lead_duplication_based_on_emails` is off), archived ones included: a new Lead with the email of an archived one is refused (`CONFLICT`, "Another Lead already uses this email address."); a Manager restores the archived Lead or the user picks another email. The Lead's owner is not told of an archive (the delete did not tell them either).
- Data: custom fields `custom_crm_archived` (Check), `custom_crm_archived_at` (Datetime) and `custom_crm_archived_by` (Link User) on Lead, installed by the custom-field sync on migrate.

## Booking

- API: `bookings.archive_booking(id)` and `bookings.unarchive_booking(id)` (POST, Manager); `bookings.list_bookings` and its summary take `archiveState`; `bookings.get_booking(id)` reads one (the Booking page reads by id, since an archived Booking is not in the pipeline); the Booking carries `archived`, and `archivedAt` and `archivedBy` while archived.
- Only an Upsell Booking that is not completed is archived. An Opportunity Booking is the PO record behind its Opportunity (the live booking, rollback and loss rules rely on it) and is only ever cancelled; a completed Booking (a service delivered, an order converted to an Installed Tool, which names it) is the record of a delivered order (`INVALID_TRANSITION` for both; the delete refused the first, and most completed ones: those an Installed Tool or a notice names).
- The Booking page reads its record by id; a Booking the reader cannot see is "not found" to them, as it was when it was not in their pipeline.
- An archived Booking is refused every change (`INVALID_TRANSITION`, "a Manager can restore it from the archive"): an edit, the actual PO date and the CNY amount, a service completion, a conversion to an Installed Tool. It is out of the Booking list and summary (active by default; the pipeline, the dashboard and the Contact 360 read the list), the Account 360 (which reads the list unpaginated, active ones alone), the deadline reminders and the CNY backfill report. Review counts only an Opportunity's own Bookings, which are never archived.
- Data: fields `archived` (Check), `archived_at` (Datetime) and `archived_by` (Link User) on CRM Booking, installed by the doctype sync on migrate.

## Opportunity

- API: `opportunities.archive_strategy_opportunity(id)` and `opportunities.unarchive_strategy_opportunity(id)` (POST, Manager); `opportunities.list_strategy_opportunities` takes `archiveState`; the Opportunity carries `archived`, and `archivedAt` and `archivedBy` while archived; its history holds an `archived` row for each archive and restore.
- Refused (`INVALID_TRANSITION`) while a Booking of its own is not cancelled (cancel the Booking first; a completed order cannot be cancelled, so a delivered deal stays, as it could not be deleted), a Demo Request that has not ended (cancel it first) or a demo test that has not ended (finish or terminate it first). The archive reads them under the Opportunity's lock, as committed by then, so none of them is in progress once it is archived: no order to deliver, no Demo Request in a review, no demo test running, and nothing left to remind of. An internal validation or a quotation approval waiting is frozen with it (no decision until the restore), not waited for; an RFQ goes on up to its approval, which is refused (an RFQ has no end the archive could wait for).
- An archived Opportunity is refused every change (`INVALID_TRANSITION`, "cannot be changed"): an edit, a stage action (budget, internal validation, PO, marking lost), a rollback, a stakeholder, an action, a recovery plan or action, a post-mortem (its loss category too), a status record, a scorecard link, its red flags or the Blue Sheet's suggested level (its page offers neither), a warning's next step. Its Quotations are frozen with it: no draft saved or discarded, no new version, no submission, approval (either way) or publication, no customer decision, no send plan or follow-up, until the restore; the Quotation says its Opportunity is archived (`opportunityArchived`), and its page offers none of those writes. An RFQ approval, which would move the Opportunity on, is refused at that step. It takes no new record naming it ("takes no new record"): a Visit, a Demo Request, a Quotation (created or linked), an RFQ, a Booking, an Event (created, or made to name it); each of those creations reads the flag again under the Opportunity's lock (a Quotation, created or linked, before its row is written; an RFQ after its row and its files are written, which a refusal takes back out, the files' bytes included), so an archive in flight is waited for and one committed since the first read is seen. A Quotation's publication reads it under the lock after its PDF is written (no file's content lock is taken while the Opportunity's row is held), the PDF taken back out on a refusal. Its own Bookings (cancelled, since it is archived) take no change either. Its Demo Requests have all ended (the archive waits for them): they take no step, and keep their comments and Word copies as any ended one does. It is out of the Opportunity list and board, the Opportunity Board (which has no archive filter), the pipeline (the Account and Contact 360 with it), the dashboard (which reads the deals and their actions itself), the Account's figures, the cockpit (Review's figures of the deal: its stage, amount, stages, actions, plans, probability and Lead conversion; Warnings; Plan's open deals and actions, and its Quotations to send and their follow-ups, as a closed deal's; a quotation of its own is no expiry warning) and the warning notices. The Visits, Quotations (their follow-ups done too), Events and demo tests that name it are records of their own: they keep counting where they count (Review's activity facts: the Visits, the Quotations and their follow-ups done, the demo tests), and a planned Visit or Event naming it, the Account's, stays in Plan (an Event naming it is still kept up, as a Visit is).
- Notices and warning handling already written about it stay (they are the users' history; a notice opens the read-only page). The warning job reads the slip and legacy stage-6 rows again under the Opportunity's lock before it writes a notice, so a parent archived after the job's snapshot gets none of those; a lost, overdue, quotation-expiry or stage-6-with-history notice of the job's snapshot can still be written once.
- Data: fields `archived` (Check), `archived_at` (Datetime) and `archived_by` (Link User) on CRM Opportunity, installed by the doctype sync on migrate.
